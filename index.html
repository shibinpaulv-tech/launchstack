03 / how we work

        
You see it working before we build it.

        
Every project starts with a prototype you can click through on your own phone, so there are no surprises later.

      

      

        

        
step 1
Talk
A free call or visit. We learn how your business runs and what needs fixing.

        
step 2
Prototype
A clickable version of your app or site. You try it and change it until it feels right.

        
step 3
Build and launch
We build the real thing, test it with you and put it live.

        
step 4
Support
Fixes, updates and new features as you grow. One phone call away.
